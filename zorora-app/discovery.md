# Discovery

Satellite-based geospatial map with 1,144 mineral deposits, 739 generation assets, mining concessions, and transmission infrastructure. Click any asset and get its capacity, technology, ownership, and construction status — then launch a diligence search or research report directly from the map.

## Key Features

- **Sentinel-2 satellite imagery with VIIRS night-lights** — see generation activity from space
- **739 generation assets** with capacity, operator, technology, and status
- **1,144 mineral deposits and mining concessions** — track resource extraction
- **Transmission infrastructure** — grid lines and substations
- **One-click diligence** — launch a research report or diligence search from any asset popup
