# Digest

Stage articles and market datasets from [Global View](/zorora-app/global-view) or [Deep Research](/zorora-app/deep-research), then synthesize them into structured energy market and policy digests you can forward to your investment committee.

## Key Features

- **Stage articles and datasets** from Global View, Deep Research, or external sources
- **Synthesized market briefs** — Nehanda v3 generates a structured summary with citations
- **Shareable output** — forward digests to your investment committee or stakeholders
- **Topic clustering** — related articles are grouped thematically

---

## Workflow

1. Browse articles and commodity data in [Global View](/zorora-app/global-view)
2. Stage items of interest into the digest queue
3. Click "Synthesize" to generate a structured market brief
4. Review the synthesized digest with inline citations
5. Share or export the digest
