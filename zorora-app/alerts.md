# Alerts

Monitor topics and sources for new developments with configurable alert rules. Get notified when new regulatory rulings, market data updates, or news articles match your criteria — before your competitors find them.

## Key Features

- **Configurable alert rules** — define triggers by topic, source, jurisdiction, or keyword
- **Regulatory alerts** — notified when new NERSA, ZERA, or FERC rulings arrive
- **Market data alerts** — trigger on commodity price thresholds or SAPP DAM movements
- **News alerts** — new articles matching your topics from the geo-tagged feed
- **Delivery** — in-app notifications and optional email delivery
