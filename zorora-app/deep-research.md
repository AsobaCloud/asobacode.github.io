# Deep Research

Ask Zorora a research question and get a structured report with inline citations, revenue projections, and embedded charts — not a chatbot response you can't trace back to a source. Every claim cites its source. When sources disagree, Zorora tells you exactly where and why.

Powered by [Nehanda v3](/nehanda-model) — a 27B parameter model trained on 490+ energy policy documents to refuse fabrication, not optimize for plausibility.

## Key Features

- **Structured reports with inline source citations** — every claim links to its source document
- **Credibility-scored sources** — high-confidence evidence ranks first
- **Revenue estimates, tariff structures, and regulatory context** — financial modeling built into the report
- **Follow-up questions grounded in the same source material** — maintain context across a research session
- **Diligence search** — structured data from EIA, utility rate, and World Bank databases for brownfield acquisition due diligence
