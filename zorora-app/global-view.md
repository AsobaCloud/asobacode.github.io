# Global View

Live commodity pricing alongside treasury rates and geo-tagged article feeds broken down by country and topic. Click a country and see what's being written about it: economy, geopolitics, energy policy, AI, prediction markets. Stage any commodity dataset or article into a digest and get a synthesized market brief.

## Key Features

- **Interactive country map** — click any country for geo-tagged articles by topic
- **Live commodity pricing** — WTI, Brent, Henry Hub, uranium, and more (FRED data, daily updates)
- **Treasury rates** — US, SA, and ZW government bonds
- **Geo-tagged articles** across 169 countries with topic breakdown (economy, geopolitics, energy policy, AI, prediction markets)
- **Polymarket prediction data** for geopolitical event tracking
- **Stage to digest** — send any dataset or article to [Digest](/zorora-app/digest) for synthesis
