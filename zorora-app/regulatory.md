# Regulatory Monitoring

Track the rulings that move markets. 174 cached regulatory events across South Africa, Zimbabwe, and the US — NERSA tariff decisions, ZERA licensing notices, FERC orders, and state RPS compliance data.

A NERSA notice about municipal tariff redetermination for Ekurhuleni, Msunduzi, or Madibeng changes the economics of every asset in those service areas. Zorora surfaces these events automatically — before your competitors find them in the gazette.

## Key Features

- **174 regulatory events cached** with provenance tracking
- **3,264 US RPS compliance records** across all states
- **Filter by jurisdiction, state, fuel type, and year**
- **Configurable alerts** when new rulings arrive
- **Coverage**: NERSA (South Africa), ZERA (Zimbabwe), FERC & state RPS (United States)
