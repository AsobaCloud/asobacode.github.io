# Zorora App

Zorora is an energy intelligence platform for African energy markets. It provides deep research, acquisition diligence, regulatory monitoring, and market intelligence — powered by [Nehanda v3](/nehanda-model), a 27B parameter model trained on 490+ energy policy documents.

## Seven Integrated Modes

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| [Deep Research](/zorora-app/deep-research) | Cited research reports with credibility scoring and revenue projections |
| [Discovery](/zorora-app/discovery) | Satellite-based geospatial map with 1,144 mineral deposits and 739 generation assets |
| [Regulatory](/zorora-app/regulatory) | Track NERSA, ZERA, and FERC rulings across jurisdictions |
| [Global View](/zorora-app/global-view) | Live commodity pricing, treasury rates, and geo-tagged news by country |
| [Digest](/zorora-app/digest) | Stage articles and datasets into synthesized market briefs |
| [Alerts](/zorora-app/alerts) | Monitor topics and sources with configurable alert rules |
| [Scouting](/zorora-app/scouting) | Kanban pipeline for tracking brownfield, greenfield, and BESS opportunities |

Plus [Settings](/zorora-app/settings) for API keys, model configuration, and preferences.

## Data Sources

Zorora pulls from the same regulatory feeds, market data providers, and academic databases your team queries manually — all in one place:

- **SAPP** — DAM prices: RSA-N, RSA-S, ZW (hourly)
- **Eskom** — System demand, RE gen, station build-up (27 series)
- **NERSA** — Tariff rulings, utility licensing, IPP Office filings
- **ZERA** — Generation licensing, electricity pricing (ZW)
- **Ember Energy** — SA & ZW coal, wind, solar gen (monthly)
- **FRED** — Oil, gas, uranium, FX (ZAR/USD, ZMW/USD)
- **World Bank** — SADC electricity: access, T&D loss, coal %
- **NASA POWER** — Solar irradiance, wind, temp (site scoring)

**80+ live market series** across 6 providers, plus 490+ energy policy documents in the Nehanda v3 training corpus.

## Why Zorora Exists

Energy projects fail for reasons that don't show up in financial models — grid connection delays, municipal tariff disputes, environmental permitting gaps, counterparty exposure to contractors that lost accreditation. The risks that kill energy deals are regulatory, political, and operational.

Zorora surfaces the specific non-financial factors that determine whether a project reaches commercial operation, from government gazettes, NERSA notices, and municipal filings that no spreadsheet can aggregate.
