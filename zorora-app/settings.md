## Configuration Options

- **API keys** — manage keys for data sources (SAPP, FRED, etc.)
- **Model selection** — choose between Nehanda v3 model endpoints
- **Default jurisdictions** — set your preferred regulatory jurisdictions
- **Alert delivery** — configure email notification preferences
- **Display preferences** — timezone, currency, and units
